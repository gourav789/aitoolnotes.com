Refund & Cancellation Policy

Refund rules for the digital course.

This policy applies to the digital courses purchased on AI Tool Notes (aitoolnotes.com).

1. Nature Of The Product

AI Tool Notes is a digital educational product. The full course content unlocks immediately after payment and the user gets complete access right away. Because of this, the product is an "instantly delivered digital good".

2. Refund Rule

Since course access is granted immediately after payment and the content cannot be "returned", payments are generally non-refundable.

We strongly recommend watching the free demo videos before buying — they are available with every course on the landing page — so that you get a clear idea of the content.

3. Exceptions (When A Refund May Be Issued)

We consider a refund in the following situations:
